Title: Vastu Ke 5 Zaruri Niyam
Date: 01 June 2025
Category: Vastu

Vastu Shastra mein kuch basic niyam hain jo aapke ghar mein positive energy laate hain:

1. Ghar ka main darwaza uttar ya poorv disha mein hona chahiye.
2. Rasoi (kitchen) hamesha aagneya (south-east) disha mein banwayen.
3. Puja ghar ishaan (north-east) kone mein rakhein.
4. Sone ka kamra nairutya (south-west) mein hona shubh hota hai.
5. Bathroom aur toilet vayavya (north-west) ya agneya mein banwayen.

Zyada jaankari ke liye Acharya Anuj se sampark karein.
